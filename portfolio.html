Research-minded, technically grounded

I'm a Computer Science graduate and ICT professional currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Computer Science at Rivers State University, Port Harcourt. I've delivered remote technical support, AI model training and evaluation, and research assistance for both international and local organizations.
